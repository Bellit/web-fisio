## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, one self-contained `index.html` per proposal (`propuesta-N/`), no build step, so each proposal opens by double-click and deploys to any static host. (Inferred: the user asked not to be questioned.)

## Users

People in Barcelona and its metropolitan area looking for a physiotherapist: mostly women aged 25–55, many searching from their phone after a recommendation or an Instagram profile. Typical situations: back/neck pain from desk work, sports injuries, pregnancy and postpartum, recovery after (often aesthetic) surgery, jaw tension. Their job on the site: find out who she is, whether she treats their problem, where she is (or whether she comes home), what it costs, and book. (Inferred from the brief.)

## Product Purpose

Personal website for one independent physiotherapist, not a clinic. It must show who she is, where she works, her specialities, and let visitors book an appointment. Success = booking requests (form or WhatsApp).

## Positioning

A single, recognisable professional with a strong personal style — premium, fashionable, "diva" — who treats few patients a day with full 50-minute sessions, both in her own studio and at the patient's home. Neighbouring clinics cannot copy the personal brand or the studio-or-home choice with one person.

## Operating Context

- Studio in the Eixample (Barcelona) plus home visits in Barcelona city and the metropolitan area; also hotels.
- Bookings are confirmed by WhatsApp. No booking backend chosen yet.
- Visitors arrive mostly on mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Specialities: pelvic floor and postpartum, sports injuries, back and neck, lymphatic drainage and post-op, jaw/TMJ, myofascial and dry needling, plus initial assessment.
- All proposals are design proposals: booking flows are demos, no data is sent.
- Copy in Spanish (Spain).

## Brand Commitments

- Name used in proposals: Gala Ferrer (invented; client to confirm), woman, 30.
- Personality: diva, loves sparkle and premium things, very stylish and fashion-forward.
- User feedback, binding: must feel very premium and modern; pastel palettes welcome, combinations of three colours; tone must be professional (no "reina", no colloquial flirting); rejected looks: 2005-era magazine (oxblood + gold + Bodoni) and holographic night glam that reads as tarot/psychic.

## Product Principles

1. She is the brand: her face, voice and judgment lead every page.
2. Booking is never more than one tap away, on every viewport.
3. Studio or home is a first-class choice, never a footnote.
4. Premium through restraint and finish, not through ornament.
5. Professional clarity first: a patient must understand what is treated, how long, and for how much.
