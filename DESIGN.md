# Design System: Gala Ferrer, fisioterapia (four proposals)

This file records four parallel visual worlds built for one client. None of them is the house system yet: the client chooses one, and at that point the other three sections get archived and the chosen one becomes the whole file. Until then every token carries its proposal prefix (`p1-`, `p4-`, `p5-`, `p6-`) and must not be mixed across worlds. The index page (`index.html`) is a neutral selector (Figtree on `#f4f1f5`, white cards) and is not a fifth world.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One person, four rooms"**

Every proposal sells the same person and the same booking flow, each in its own room. The shared floor is product, not style: who Gala is, what she treats, studio or home as a first-class choice, prices in plain view, and a booking that is never more than one tap away. Everything visual sits inside one proposal.

The four rooms, as built (P2 Atelier and P3 Joyero were discarded by the client on 2026-11-17; prefixes keep their original numbers):

- **P1 Pastel** (`propuesta-1/`): an airy light page in a single light-weight geometric sans (Urbanist 300). It has a soft bento of rounded tiles, three switchable three-colour pastel combos, and a 4-step booking card set on a pastel gradient.
- **P4 Lookbook** (`propuesta-4/`): a fashion-brand lookbook. A giant lowercase Syne name fills the hero with a tilted portrait card, a rotating word pill completes the sentence, an ink ribbon marquee lists specialities, the specialities are full-width flat-pastel cards that stack and recede on scroll, studio and home are two equal panels (ink and white), and booking is a month calendar with a sticky summary. DM Sans for body.
- **P5 Stories** (`propuesta-5/`): the site as tap-through vertical story frames inside a phone with a bezel and island on desktop (bare rounded viewer on mobile). App gestures: 3D cube between stories, hold to pause, horizontal swipe, double tap for a heart burst. Stickers: a live countdown to the next free slot, a pain slider on the body map that feeds the WhatsApp message. Flat solid ground and ink rings; Hanken Grotesk 800 throughout.
- **P6 Producto** (`propuesta-6/`, landing): an Apple-style product page. White, mist and black sections, Inter Tight display and Inter body, one rose for action. Scroll-driven moments: the portrait card grows to full bleed, a manifesto lights up word by word, a sticky body figure glows on each speciality, counters run once. Booking is a buy-page configurator (where, treatment, day, hour, data) with a frosted summary bar.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Four self-contained worlds, each in one HTML file with its tokens declared in `:root`.
- Every world pairs a pastel or soft ground with one deep ink, and that ink carries the primary action.
- Every world uses pill buttons (999px) with a minimum height of 44 to 52px.
- Booking is a demo in all four, and placeholder data is labelled in the page.

### Shared floor (all four)

- **Content model:** the same Gala Ferrer, the same seven specialities plus initial assessment, the same studio at Carrer de València 284 plus home visits, and the same price set (65 € studio, 90 € home in Barcelona, 100 € metro, 5-session pack for 300 €). Every one of these values is a placeholder from PRODUCT.md.
- **Booking semantics:** choose a treatment, choose studio or home (home asks for an address and a postcode that must match `^08\d{3}$`), choose a day and a free hour (busy hours are disabled and struck through), then give name, phone (9 digits) and privacy consent. A hidden honeypot field (`.hp`) is present. Nothing is sent: P1, P4 and P6 end on a confirmation, and P5 opens `wa.me` with the composed text.
- **Accessibility floor:** a skip link to `#main`, a 2px `:focus-visible` outline in the world's ink with a 3px offset, touch targets of at least 44px, selected states exposed with `aria-pressed`, `aria-current` or `:checked`, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` rule that kills transitions, animations and smooth scroll (P5 also starts its stories paused). The error text colour is `shared-error` (P6 uses `p6-error`).
- **Placeholder rule:** name, address, phone, email, collegiate number, prices, hours and training are marked "dato de ejemplo" inline or in the footer. Photo slots are tinted planes with a "Foto de Gala, pendiente" pill. No testimonials, review counts or press logos exist and none may be added.

## Colors

Each world has its own palette. Primary means the ink that carries action; the pastels are grounds and fills.

### Primary
- **P1 Aubergine Ink** (p1-ink): primary buttons, the selected day, the highlighted price card and the current booking step. The deep of each combo (p1-*-deep) marks checked choices and completed steps.
- **P4 Violet Ink** (p4-ink): primary buttons, the studio panel, the ribbon, the selected day and slot, the footer. p4-accent marks free days in the calendar and the focus ring.
- **P5 Story Ink** (p5-ink): buttons, the progress fill, the reply send disc, the avatar and the selected poll option.
- **P6 Rose** (p6-rose): every button, link and selected option ring. p6-rose-light is the glow and price accent on black.

### Secondary
- **P1 three combos**: Peonía (c1 pink, c2 apricot, c3 lavender), Salvia (sage, blush, sky) and Lila (lilac, pink, mint). A fixed palette chooser swaps all four variables, and every surface reads them.
- **P4 four flat pastels**: lavanda, rosa, menta and melocotón cycle through the stacked cards (`nth-child(4n+…)`), and also tint the rotating word, the portrait and the panel tags.
- **P5 story pigments**: powder, lilac, peach and sage. Each frame gets one flat pigment, and the highlight discs cycle through them.
- **P6 Pink** (p6-pink, p6-pink-2): photo planes, icon tiles and the confirmation disc. Never a page ground.

### Neutral
- **P1** background `#fdfbfc` with a 12% ink hairline (p1-line) on cards and fields. Soft ink is for secondary text.
- **P4** cool paper `#f6f5f8` page, white for the booking section, fact cards and home panel, `#dedbe6` hairline.
- **P5** a flat `#f6e4e7` ground. White `#fffafb` is stickers, rows and fields. The phone bezel is `#241d29`.
- **P6** white page, mist `#f5f5f7` alternate sections, pure black for the specialities chapter, `#d2d2d7` hairlines.

### Named Rules
**The Proposal Prefix Rule.** A token belongs to one world. A P4 pigment never appears in P6, and the shared floor owns no colour except the error red.

**The Live Combo Rule (P1).** Pastel fills reference the switchable variables (`--c1..c3`), never literal hexes, so the palette chooser repaints the whole page.

**The No Gradient Rule (P4, P5).** The client rejected gradients as dated. Colour is flat; the only soft falloff left in the set is P6's speciality glow.

**The Rose Is Action Rule (P6).** Rose appears only on things you can press or on the active state; pink is reserved for imagery planes.


## Typography

| World | Display | Body |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Urbanist 300 | Urbanist 400 |
| P4 | Syne 600/700, lowercase in the hero and footer | DM Sans 400/500/600 |
| P5 | Hanken Grotesk 800 | Hanken Grotesk 400/500/600 |
| P6 | Inter Tight 500/600 | Inter 400/500/600 |

All fonts load from Google Fonts with `display=swap`. Body text is 1.0625rem in every world, with line height between 1.55 and 1.65.

**Character:**
- **P1:** a whisper-weight sans with tight negative tracking.
- **P4:** a wide fashion grotesk set huge and tight, like a lookbook masthead.
- **P5:** a single bold sans, as in story captions and stickers.
- **P6:** a tight neo-grotesk with short declarative sentences ending in a full stop, as on a product page.

### Hierarchy
- **Display:** the h1 or hero claim only. Sizes are in the frontmatter `p*-display` entries. P5's display is the right-hand column claim; inside the frame the h1 is the profile name at 1.35rem/800.
- **Headline:** section h2s (`p*-headline`). In P5 the in-frame h2 is the headline and scales with viewport height (`3.4vh`) because the frame does.
- **Title:** card, piece, tile and row titles (`p*-title`).
- **Body:** 1.0625rem everywhere. Measure is capped at 40 to 60ch in leads and paragraphs.
- **Label:** 0.78 to 0.92rem at weight 500/600 for slot meta, field labels and step names. No world uses uppercase tracked labels.

### Named Rules
**The Light Display Rule (P1).** Display stays at weight 300/400. Hierarchy comes from size and negative tracking, never from bold.

**The Giant Name Rule (P4).** The name is the image: lowercase Syne at up to 15.5rem with −0.055em tracking, in the hero and again in the footer.

**The Caption Highlight Rule (P5).** The right-column claim sits on white highlight boxes (`box-decoration-break: clone`, 0.18em radius), which is the story text mode. It appears only in that column.

## Layout

- **P1:** the container is `min(1200px, 100% - 2rem)`. The hero is a 1.1 / 0.9 split. Specialities sit in a 4-column bento with 220px minimum rows and a 2x2 lead tile, dropping to 2 columns at 960px and 1 at 600px. Sections are padded `clamp(4rem, 8vw, 7rem)`.
- **P4:** the container is 1240px. The hero is the two-line name with the second line indented, a tilted 3:4 portrait absolutely placed top-right (static and full width below 900px), and a 2-column row of intro and actions. Section heads are a 1 / 1 grid with the paragraph right-aligned. Specialities are sticky cards (`top: 96px + i × 18px`) whose predecessor scales to 0.95 and darkens as the next one covers it. Booking is a 1.3 / 1 split with a sticky summary.
- **P5:** the stage is a grid of `auto` plus `1fr`. The viewer is 9:16, `calc(clamp(600px, 100vh - 230px, 780px) * 9 / 16)` wide, with a 10px bezel, 46px outer corners and a 96×26 island; frame content starts 40px lower to clear it. Highlights sit above it in a horizontal row. Below 900px the right column drops under, the viewer fills `min(100svh - 120px, …)` up to 440px wide, the profile collapses to the name, and the highlight rings shrink to 56px.
- **P6:** the container is 1020px and everything is centred like a product page. Two pinned chapters use tall sections with sticky 100vh stages: the portrait zoom (230vh) and the manifesto (240vh). The specialities chapter is a 1 / 1 split with a sticky figure and 62vh steps (figure pinned on top below 760px). Ways are two equal cards; facts are a 4-column row (2 below 760px); booking is a 1 / 1.6 question grid with locked rows that unlock in order.

## Elevation & Depth

The worlds are mostly flat. Depth comes from tonal layering and paper-on-ground contrast. Shadows exist only where the world's own object casts one.

- **P1:** one ambient shadow, under the floating palette chooser (`0 8px 24px -12px rgba(43,37,48,.3)`). The sticky header uses an 86% background with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`, and the hero slot card uses blur(10px).
- **P4:** flat. Depth is the stacking cards (scale and brightness on scroll) and the blurred sticky header.
- **P5:** a device shadow on the desktop viewer (`0 30px 60px -30px rgba(42,34,49,.7)` plus a 1.5px rim), a phone lift on mobile (`0 18px 40px -24px rgba(42,34,49,.55)`) and a sticker lift on stickers and the fact card (`0 6px 16px -12px rgba(42,34,49,.55)`).
- **P6:** no shadows. Depth is section tone (white, mist, black) and the frosted nav and summary bar (`saturate(180%) blur(20px)`).

### Named Rules
**The Stack Rule (P4).** Speciality cards are the only moving surfaces; they never get drop shadows, only scale and brightness as they are covered.

**The Sticker Lift Rule (P5).** Only objects that sit on top of a story (the viewer, stickers, the fact card) get a shadow, and it is always the same short, negative-spread ink shadow.

## Shapes

- **P1:** soft and round. There are 40px (hero photo, booking wrap), 28px (tiles, places, prices, booking card), 18px (credentials, choices, days, summary) and 14px (fields) radii. Portrait discs are full circles, and the logo is a conic three-colour dot.
- **P4:** 28px cards, panels and portrait, 20px fact cards and next-slot card, 16px fields, circular calendar days, pills for buttons, slots, tags and the rotating word.
- **P5:** 46px device (36px inner frames; 24px bare viewer on mobile), 20px photo, 18px stickers and reply field, 14px rows and facts, 12px postcode field, and circular rings and buttons. Stickers rotate −1.5deg and the fact card −1deg.
- **P6:** 28px cards and photo planes (the zoom shot shrinks its radius from 36px to 0 as it fills the screen), 24px icon tiles, 16px options, 14px fields.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** pill (999px) in every world, at least 48 to 52px tall (44px in compact header and in-card use).
- **Primary:** the world's ink fill with paper text.
- **Hover:** a one-step lighter ink (`#40384a` P1, `#363149` P4, `#43384b` P5); P6 darkens rose to `#a8335b`. P1 also lifts 1px with `translateY(-1px)`. P4 and P6 scale to 0.97 on press.
- **Secondary:** white or paper fill (P1 soft with a hairline, P5 soft white), a line button (P4 with a 1.5px ink border, P6 with a 1.5px rose border), or P6's rose text link with a chevron.
- **Disabled (P6):** the summary-bar button greys to `#d2d2d7` until the four choices are made.

### Chips / Slots
- **P1:** hairline pills. Selected takes c1 with a deep border.
- **P4:** white pills with a 1.5px line; chosen fills ink. Calendar days with free hours carry a 4px accent dot; days without hours are disabled.
- **P5:** poll options are 1.5px ink outline pills that invert when pressed.
- **P6:** white 16px options with a 1.5px grey border; pressed gets a rose border plus a 1px rose ring. Busy hours are disabled and struck through.

### Cards / Containers
- **P1 tiles:** 28px, filled with c1, c2, c3 or white, 1.75rem padding.
- **P4 stack cards:** full-width, 28px, a big Syne index number, title and description, price and Reservar pill on the right; pastels cycle every four.
- **P5 rows:** white rows at 14px radius inside the frames.
- **P6 way cards:** white 28px cards on mist with a pink icon tile, price and a ruled spec list, like a model comparison.

### Inputs / Fields
- **P1:** 14px radius, hairline, `#fdfbfc` fill, ink focus outline.
- **P4:** 16px radius (999px for the postcode field), 1.5px line border that turns ink on focus.
- **P5:** 12px postcode field with letter-spacing 0.08em. The reply textarea is 18px, 90% white, and auto-grows from 48px to 120px.
- **P6:** 56px tall, 14px radius, 1.5px grey border that turns rose with a 1px ring on focus.

### Navigation
- **P1, P4:** inline text links in soft ink. Hover darkens to ink. The links hide below 860px (P1) or 820px (P4), and the header keeps the booking pill. P4's header turns frosted after 20px of scroll.
- **P6:** a 52px frosted global nav with small grey links and a compact rose pill; links hide below 760px.
- **P5:** highlight rings are the navigation. Each is a 68px disc (56px on mobile) with a pigment fill and a stroke SVG icon, and a solid ink ring appears only on the current or hovered item.

### Booking stepper (P1)
- Four steps (Tratamiento, Lugar, Día y hora, Tus datos) as a 4-column list with 3px top bars. Below 560px, only the current step label is shown.

### Signature: P4 stacked cards and calendar
Six sticky cards stack under the header; on scroll each covered card scales by up to 5% and darkens by up to 7%. A "Próxima hora libre" card with a pulsing green dot preselects the first free slot. Booking is a Monday-first month calendar (current and next month, 45 days ahead), slot pills for the chosen day, a studio/home segmented control and a sticky summary with treatment select, total and the data form.

### Signature: P5 story viewer
- **Bars:** segmented 3px progress bars across the top, filled in ink.
- **Paging and controls:** tap zones on the left and right 22% of the frame page backwards and forwards. A pause button sits top-right.
- **Frame transition:** a 3D cube turn (`translateZ(−hw) rotateY(±90deg) translateZ(hw)`, 0.6s) with the leaving face dimming, then each frame's children rise in with a stagger.
- **Gestures:** hold a side for 260ms to pause until release, swipe more than 60px to page, double tap the middle for a heart burst that also presses the like button in the reply bar.
- **Stickers:** a live h/min/s countdown to the next free slot (frame 1), and a 0–10 pain slider with an emoji readout under the body-map answer (frame 2) that is appended to the WhatsApp text.
- **Timing:** only frames with `data-auto` advance on their own (7s, 9s and 8s), driven by `requestAnimationFrame` on elapsed time. Pressing or focusing a control inside the viewer pauses, and reduced motion starts paused and removes the cube and stagger.
- **Deep links:** each frame has an id, `pushState` deep links it, and `popstate` retraces the trail.
- **Reply bar:** the reply bar on every frame composes the WhatsApp text.

### Signature: P6 scroll chapters and configurator

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every value from the chosen proposal's own `p*-` tokens and nothing from a sibling.
- **Do** keep booking one tap away: a header pill, the hero slots (P1, P4, P5), the summary bar (P6) and the reply bar (P5).
- **Do** keep studio and home as two equal options in every booking control.
- **Do** label every placeholder value "dato de ejemplo" and every photo slot "pendiente".
- **Do** in P4 keep the specialities as one vertical stack; never a horizontal rail.
- **Do** in P6 tie motion to scroll position or to a single entrance, and give every pinned chapter a static reduced-motion layout.
- **Do** in P5 keep auto-advance pausable, pause it on interaction, and keep each frame addressable by hash.

### Don't:
- **Don't** mix fonts or pigments between proposals, and don't use P6's pink as a page ground.
- **Don't** add shadows to P4 cards. Shadows exist only where listed in Elevation & Depth.
- **Don't** use horizontal scrollers that cut cards (the client rejected a carousel rail), gradient washes, or the hydraulic-tile look (rejected P4).
- **Don't** use the looks the client rejected: 2005-era magazine (oxblood, gold and Bodoni) and holographic night glam.
- **Don't** add testimonials, review stars, patient counts or press logos. None exist.
- **Don't** add eyebrow or kicker labels above headings, glyph icons, or gradient-filled text. See the not-canonized note below.

### Not canonized (defects carried by the build, not rules)
- **P1:** the hero opens with an eyebrow pill (`.where-line`, "Fisioterapeuta en Barcelona…") led by a "✦" text glyph. It is a kicker plus a glyph icon and must not be copied.
